The Rings of Power

In Middle-earth, several powerful rings were created and given to Elves, Dwarves, and Men. However, Sauron secretly created the One Ring to increase his own power and dominate the bearers of the other rings.

The Three Elven Rings

Three Rings of Power were held by the Elves. Unlike the other rings, these were not directly touched by Sauron when they were made. They were used for preservation and protection rather than conquest.

The Seven Dwarven Rings

Seven rings were given to the Dwarf-lords. The rings increased their desire for wealth and contributed to the growth of their great treasures, but Sauron was unable to completely dominate the Dwarves through them.

The Nine Rings of Men

Nine rings were given to powerful Men. Their rings granted them power and unnaturally long lives, but they gradually fell under Sauron's control and became the Nazgûl, also known as the Ringwraiths.

The One Ring

Sauron forged the One Ring in Mount Doom. He placed a significant part of his own power into it and intended to use it to control the other Rings of Power and their bearers.

The One Ring eventually became central to the struggle for Middle-earth, as destroying it was the only way to permanently defeat Sauron's power.
